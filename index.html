Named after two of history's most iconic thinkers — Einstein, who reshaped how we understand the universe, and Socrates, who reshaped how we understand ourselves — I'm not a physicist or philosopher. But I love what their names represent: curiosity, questioning, and pushing ideas further.

That's why I chose business analytics and why I'm passionate about artificial intelligence. Analytics gives me the how. AI gives me the what's next. Together, they let me build tools and ideas that actually help people.

So while I'm not calculating relativity or debating virtue, I am using analytics + AI to build the future of business — with a name that always starts a conversation.
